Nestled along the scenic pilgrimage route linking Piravom and Koothattukulam, **Thirumarady Sri Mahadeva Temple** stands as a beacon of Sanatana Dharma with centuries of recorded antiquity.

### The Sacred Heritage & Origin
According to regional folklore and temple chronicles, the presiding East-facing Lingam was consecrated during an era when great sages and spiritual luminaries traversed the verdant lands bordering the Muvattupuzha river valley. The divine presence was revealed when local cowherds noticed divine vibrations emanating from a sacred clearing surrounded by medicinal flora.

### The Four Traditional Ooralan Illams
For centuries, the governance and spiritual patronage of the temple and the adjoining sacred grove (**Edaprakavu**) were held jointly by four esteemed Namboothiri illams:
1. **Kattampilly Illam (കാട്ടാമ്പിള്ളി)**
2. **Kizhekkillam (കിഴക്കില്ലം)**
3. **Edayattuppilly Illam (എടയാറ്റുപ്പിള്ളി)**
4. **Kanjirappilly Illam (കാഞ്ഞിരപ്പള്ളി)**

These families preserved the rituals with unfaltering dedication. Today, the administration operates in active harmony with devotee representatives and the local managing committee, ensuring long-term conservation and structured pilgrim welfare under the Tantric guidance of **Kidangassery Illam**.
