In Vishnu, Devi, or Ganesha temples, devotees perform a continuous 360-degree circle around the sanctum. However, in traditional Kerala Shiva temples like **Thirumarady**, doing a full circle is strictly prohibited due to the presence of the **Somassuthram** (the northern spout discharging Abhisheka water).

### The Four Steps of Shiva Pradakshina
1. **Begin Clockwise:** Start at the flagstaff / Nandi facing the eastern sanctum and move clockwise to the northern side.
2. **Stop at the Spout:** Upon reaching the Somassuthram, bow with folded hands without crossing or stepping over it.
3. **Turn Back Counter-Clockwise:** Retrace your steps back past the east and south, reaching the other side of the spout on the north-west.
4. **Bow and Return:** Bow again and return clockwise to complete one full cycle of Lord Shiva's circumambulation.

Observing this rule preserves the cosmic balance of Shiva-Shakthi and channels profound spiritual harmony to the devotee.
