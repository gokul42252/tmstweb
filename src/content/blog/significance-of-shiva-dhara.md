Among all the offerings rendered to Lord Shiva, none is as dear to Him as **Dhara** (continuous stream of consecrated liquid). 

### The Heat of Halahala Poison
When Lord Shiva drank the deadly *Halahala* poison to save the cosmos during the churning of the celestial ocean (Samudra Manthan), intense heat surged through His being. To soothe the Supreme Destroyer, celestial waters were showered continuously over Him.

In Kerala temples, the Dhara vessel is hung directly over the Sivalingam, allowing holy water mixed with medicinal herbs and camphor to drop continuously in a tranquil, rhythmic flow.

### Spiritual & Physical Benefits:
- Calms severe mental agitation, anxiety, insomnia, and psychological stress.
- Alleviates chronic physical burn, fevers, and neurological ailments.
- Fosters profound meditative concentration and deep inner contentment.
