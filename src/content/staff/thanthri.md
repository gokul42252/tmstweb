The Thanthri from the venerable **Kidangassery Illam** holds the supreme ecclesiastical authority over Thirumarady Mahadeva Temple. By ancestral lineage and rigorous Vedic traditions, the Thanthri conducts the consecration of deities, Kodiyettam (flag hoisting) for the annual Dhanu festival, Ashtabandha Kalasam, and resolves all Tantric queries concerning the sanctum.
