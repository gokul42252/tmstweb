Pradosham occurring on a Monday (Somavaram) is especially auspicious for worshipping Lord Shiva. 

### Ritual Sequence:
- **05:00 PM:** Temple doors open for evening prayers
- **05:30 PM:** Special Pradosha Abhishekam with milk, honey, and sandalwood
- **06:30 PM:** Majestic Deeparadhana
- **07:00 PM:** Pradosha Pradakshinam adhering to the Somassuthram guidelines
- **07:30 PM:** Athazha Pooja & Vibhuti Prasad distribution
