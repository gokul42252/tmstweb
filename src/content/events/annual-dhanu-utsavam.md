The Annual Utsavam of Thirumarady Sri Mahadeva Temple is celebrated for eight days in the Malayalam month of Dhanu (December–January), concluding with the divine **Arattu on Thiruvathira Nakshatra**.

### Festival Highlights & Schedule
- **Day 1 (Kodiyettam):** Ceremonial Tantric Flag Hoisting by the revered Thanthri from **Kidangassery Illam**, accompanied by Panchavadyam and traditional temple conches.
- **Days 2 to 6:** Daily Utsavabali, Sreebhoothabali, Ezhunnallippu with caparisoned elephants, Thayambaka by renowned percussion masters, and evening cultural programs.
- **Day 7 (Pallivetta):** The divine Royal Hunt (Pallivetta) conducted late in the night with solemn ceremonial precision and Pandi Melam.
- **Day 8 (Arattu):** The holy ritual bath of the Lord in the sacred temple waters (Ambalakkulam) on Thiruvathira day, followed by Arattu Sadya, Kodiyirakkam (flag lowering), and night Deeparadhana.
