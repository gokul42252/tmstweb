Maha Shivaratri in the Malayalam month of Kumbham is one of the most auspicious spiritual observances at Thirumarady Sri Mahadeva Temple.

### Schedule of Rituals:
- **Morning:** Maha Ganapathy Homam, Rudrabhishekam, and commencement of Laksharchana.
- **Evening:** Grand Deeparadhana with thousands of glowing earthen and bronze oil lamps.
- **Yama Poojas:** Four sacred Vedic Yama poojas performed at 09:00 PM, 12:00 Midnight, 03:00 AM, and 05:00 AM with milk, tender coconut, curd, and holy vibhuti abhishekam.
- **Devotee Fasting:** Strict Shiva Vratham observance; bilva leaves and vibhuti prasad distribution.
